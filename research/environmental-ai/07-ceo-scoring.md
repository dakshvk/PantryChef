## Conflicts adjudicated

**CEQA/CEQAnet — the Regulation agent wins. Reject.** AB 130/SB 131 creates a new hand-done
exemption test *by removing the much larger task it substitutes for*. Every project that clears the
screen generates no EIR, no consultant scope, and **no §21081.6 MMRP** — because mitigation
monitoring only exists for projects that received review and adopted mitigation. Both revenue
components sit downstream of a shrinking pool.
> **The generalizable lesson: a new compliance task created by a *deregulatory* statute is not a
> market, because the statute's purpose is to reduce total work.**

**Phase I ESA — split ruling. Whitespace claim dead; a narrowed version survives at #8.** Look at
what the four named competitors actually are: **V7 Go and Nomic are horizontal document-AI
platforms, CaseMark is legal-document AI, and only Quire is environmental-sector software.** That is
evidence the *report-drafting* layer is contested by generalist tooling — not that the
**historical-records layer** (georeferencing Sanborn maps, reading aerials for fill and tank
shadows, OCR-ing city directories for former dry cleaners) is occupied. That layer is vision +
geospatial, not text drafting, and it is where EDR/LightBox extracts the most margin reselling free
federal and Library of Congress data. **The real gate is the industry's E&O anxiety about
AI-generated Phase I content — not the competitors.**

**Zone 0 status — the agent declined to adjudicate**, noting both claims were search-index
renderings and that "final **draft** approved" is language consistent with the still-a-draft
reading. Its key insight, which survives the fact-check:

> **The Zone 0 pick does not rest on Zone 0.** California defensible-space inspection is an in-force
> obligation under PRC 4291 that agencies perform today — which is why Fire Aside already has paying
> agencies. The market is proven with or without the 0–5 ft rule. What Zone 0 changes is the
> **wedge**: a hard new requirement neither incumbent built around, creating a switching moment.
